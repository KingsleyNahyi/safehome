Working name

Safe Home Ghana

Safe, dignified shelter for people living on Ghana’s streets, and a real path back to stable lives.

Our vision

A Ghana where no one has to sleep on the street, and everyone has a safe place to rest and a path to rebuild.

Our mission

To provide safe, dignified shelter for people experiencing homelessness in Ghana, and to pair it with the care, skills and community connections they need to live independently, funded through transparent partnerships with donors.

What we believe

Dignity firstEvery person is treated as a neighbour, not a case.
Shelter is the startA bed opens the door to health, work and family reconnection.
TransparencyDonors see exactly where every cedi goes.
